Winding River Adventures

Our professionally guided white water rafting tours will be one of the greatest experiences of your life! We specialize in tailoring a one of a kind experience to your needs. Come with us and experience nature as never before.

History

What started as a love for courageous adventuring on the river has turned into a generation spanning legacy of service. Matt and Tim Barrow began this with the hope of bringing the love of the outdoors to everyone.

We first opened our doors in 1957, with a dream of helping others gain a love of nature and the river. We now offer guided tours, corporate retreat packages, family reunion trips, and even training for those wanting to become guides themselves.
