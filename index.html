Alabama

Alabama is a state in the southern United States of America. It is bordered by Tennessee to the north, Mississippi to the west, Georgia to the east, and Florida and the Gulf of Mexico to the south. Huntsville, Alabama is famously called, Rocket City, because this is where the rockets that eventually launched America into space in the 1950s were developed and built.
